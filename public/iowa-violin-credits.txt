University of Iowa solo violin

Recordings: Lawrence Fritts / University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios,
2012 violin sample collection.

Source and published terms:
https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIS.html
https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MISviolin2012.html

The source states that these recordings may be downloaded and used for any
projects, without restrictions. No purchase or individual permission request
is required under those published terms.

ABC Desk packages quiet (pp), medium (mf), and loud (ff) stereo recordings as
lossless 16-bit FLAC. Silence is trimmed with 20 ms pre/post margins; up to the
first 2.4 seconds are retained. There is no per-note normalization, noise reduction,
EQ, or tuning correction. Playback transposes the nearest recorded root and
uses crossfaded sustain loops for longer notes. These are not recorded legato
transitions or a complete model of violin bowing.

Exact source archive hash, extraction coordinates, pitch checks, and packaged
file hashes are in iowa-violin/provenance.json. Rebuild with
scripts/prepare-iowa-violin.py (Python, NumPy, soundfile) and the source ZIP
identified there.
