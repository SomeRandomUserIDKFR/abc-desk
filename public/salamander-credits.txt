ABC Desk downloads only the samples needed for a performance. The recordings
are not bundled. Playback transposes samples using the source key mapping,
selects among all 16 recorded velocity layers, and applies ABC Desk's mix,
timing, release, pan, and optional room processing.

This is a sample-based adaptation, not a complete implementation of the
source instrument's SFZ/ARIA controls. Recorded release/pedal-noise layers,
sympathetic resonance, and the SFZ controller curves are not implemented.
